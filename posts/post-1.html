The dignity of labor and the ideocracy of mediocre.

August 15, 2026

Often times we look at the pity end, but then we failed to realize why people are the way they are, pity and mediocre. The issue is not with the dignity of labor, rather the mediocracy in completing the job. The reason for the failure in our society is not because the job is frown upon, we need to talk about the subject. What gives, on the laborer that does a mediocre job, and took no pride in accomplishing the job at hand. For example, do we ever question a sweeper quality of work? Yes, their job is to sweep, quite easy right, and quite replaceable right, and a shit pay correct? At the end of the day, we want a clear room or road or whatever the sweeper has done their job. However, often time we see mediocracy in the job done. Now, if we think about it, do they warrant a better pay? This quite look like a chicken and egg problem. However, I beg to differ. We are a society that doesnt take pride in what we do. This is not about the dignity of labor, rather a dignity of self. Forget about what happened in the pass, we are not going to anything out of digging in the past, but if we take the problem at hand and try to resolve this, here is how it should go.

Take pride in what you do!

Let it be a nickel paid, but do the best that you can and feel the accomplishment. If this spreads, I am sure in near future sweepers will not be taken for granted and then the pay will automatically follow. This is one simple example, and a lot to be argued, but my point remains the same. Regardless of the job at hand, how well you are compensated, or anything that follows as a reward. First reward yourself by showing the best in yourself for the tasks at hand. No one cares if you are good at it, the only thing that matter is that you do it with your whole heart, and carry that light in the next job of yours. Don’t wait for the perfect job so that you can give the best. In a world where you have to proof yourself first, how do you proof if the previous jobs that you did were all mediocre.

Signing out for the day.
