The actionable, timebound strategy.

August 1, 2026

Tomorrow, we will be celebrating the Independence day, I am sure resolution will be passed and what not. However, I see the problem with our people on this. That, we never have a resolution that is time bound, actionable or in other words, a well research implementation. We are all dreamers, but are we trying to realize it correctly? Today, I would like to share an example of how we can realize this. As far my observation is concern, we learn leadership qualities in school, Church, then move on to become Student Union leader, and Politics being the final goal. A very great road map to become a solid leader. The opportunity to mold and become a leader is plenty, but today we are facing a shortage of strong leader. Let’s examine how Leaders are shaped based on the road map above. During school days, I believe we are presented with one of the best guidance to become a strong leader. In a typical school, a class leader is guided by class teacher and entrust certain level or responsibilities, which are actionable, and time bound. For example, in a class one of the teachers is absent or yet to come in due to various reason, the class leader is responsible to notify this to concern teacher. Here the leader learns the responsible leadership skill. Let’s translate this to a better yet organizing a school, say teacher’s day. In our typical settings, all the student leaders gather together under the guidance of a teacher and then plan out the program. This will involve the collection needed, the audition, the presentation and refreshment and all the good things. Teacher’s day celebration is time bound. Everything has to be executed on the particular day, and all the program has to be executed in an order that leads to the success of the subsequent orders. Meaning, audition has to be properly conducted so that only the best can perform, which ultimately leads to successful. Now, that we have looked at the example of this, let’s translate this to a real issue example that we have been tackling. Let’s go straight to alcohol banning, which for the most part has been failing terribly but whenever a new CSO leaders are elected, they seems to be too focus on this, to ban alcohol and fail miserably again. Now, let’s say we have a society call Alcohol banning club. What should be the action of the leader when the discussion for the same is tabled? The first table should be focus on how to implement alcohol band duh. So, elect a bunch of young, capable youth and start a drive. The action start with checking vehicle, going to well known local acohol brewery and crack down. It works for a few weeks, and then the volunteers don’t have the time and slowly the drive dies down. So, back to abundance of alcohol. First failure. The leadership are not content with this. So, a second meeting is organized. What should be the leader responsibility? They should change the strategy. Because doing the same thing again and again, and expecting a different result is insanity. This time, the drive is to fine them heavily, and lets put in shaming the offerder pubically, because why not, we dont care about the social stigma the childer will carry when their parents are shamed publicaly. The drive goes on, collected logs of fine, and now the society are blessed with money to drive the alcohol band. However, down the line, the volunteers are pressured by various elements, such as the very own people that drinks. Remember, this is not a well establish that has the well funded resources and structure to drive this in long term. So the volunteers stop dropping. The seller also finds ways to recoup their losses by passing on the losses directly to the consumer. Failed drive. So, the leader has to commence another meeting to understand how to drive this forward. This time the leader dont want to focus on banning directly, but want to start with research. So, he form two groups. One group to extensively research on the best way to ban alcohol ban, and the other group to research on why alchold ban never worked. The research findings are to be presented on certain date, have a healthy debate on the pros and cons of alcohol ban. Now, this is better strategy. Here the decision will be based on well research, where the number speaks, where experience speaks. The decision on the finding solely lies on the leader. But what we are seeing today with our leader is actioning thing here and there without proper research, without proper strategy and the failure keeps mounting. Failure is not the problem, but not learning from the failure is the problem. The lack of taking decision based on well research document is what is causing our leader to be in bad books. Think about this.
